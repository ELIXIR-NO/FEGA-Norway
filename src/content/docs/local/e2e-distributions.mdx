The e2e runner ships one binary per target environment, and each binary is a distribution with
its own stages. Pick the one you mean to run.

<TileGrid label="Go runner" sublabel="(one binary per environment)">
	<Tile eyebrow="Mocked stack" title="e2e-fega" href="../e2e/go-fega/">
		Nine stages against the local compose stack. What CI runs on every push, and what
		`./dev.sh start` gives you with no setup at all.
	</Tile>
	<Tile eyebrow="Live staging" title="e2e-egadev" href="../e2e/go-egadev/">
		Six stages against `egadev.uio.no`. Runs on a host as well as in the container, and needs
		real credentials and key paths.
	</Tile>
	<Tile eyebrow="Not implemented" title="e2e-gdi" href="../e2e/go-gdi/">
